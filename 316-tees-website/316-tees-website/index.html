About

316 Tees was birthed from (John 3:16), this subtly suggests wearing and living out one’s faith without being overly explicit.

We believe clothing is more than fabric, it is a platform for purpose. Every piece is designed to inspire confidence, and remind people that what they wear can reflect what they stand for.

This is the foundation of the 316 identity, reminding people that the greatest message ever shared is one of God’s love and salvation.

Mission statement

To create high-quality, comfortable, and stylish apparel that empowers people to express their faith, values, and identity with confidence. We are committed to excellence, authenticity, and inspiring boldness through every piece we produce.

Vision statement

To become a leading faith-inspired apparel brand that transforms everyday clothing into a powerful expression of purpose, inspiring people across communities and generations to wear their message boldly.
